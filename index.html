BSc Thesis · AE3200 Design Synthesis · 2026

Dronado — “Spin It Up”

Collaborative cable-suspended payload transport

Three autonomous fixed-wing UAVs with rotatable propellers, flying in formation to carry one 50 kg cable-suspended payload over 20 km. They take off vertically, then transition to fixed-wing cruise — the efficiency of a wing without needing a runway. The point is places conventional logistics can't reach: remote rural communities and disaster-hit regions, which we designed against a Hurricane Maria relief scenario.

What makes it hard is that no airframe owns the load. Three aircraft share one suspended mass through cables, so the formation has to hold under coordinated trajectory optimisation, with redundant sensing and comms behind it.

  • Simulink
  • Trajectory Optimisation
  • Optimal Control

Coursework · UC3M Madrid · 2025

FungEye

Mushroom genus classification from photographs

Final project for Artificial Intelligence in Organizations during my exchange in Madrid. Classifies nine mushroom genera from images, with a tenth “unknown” bucket for low-confidence predictions rather than a forced guess. Two approaches trained and compared — MobileNetV2 with ImageNet transfer learning, and YOLOv8m-cls — plus scraping to extend the dataset and GAN-generated images to cover the thin classes.
